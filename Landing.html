Evidence model

AI can analyze the evidence. It doesn't own the decision.

ClipNexus separates what came from the source from what the system derives from it.

Source-observed

Information directly represented by the transcript or acquisition provider.

  • Transcript text
  • Original timing information
  • Speaker attribution when available
  • Provider and acquisition provenance
  • Source limitations and uncertainty

Derived analysis

Information produced by downstream processing or AI analysis.

  • Points of interest
  • Event relationships
  • Context requirements
  • Potential clip boundaries
  • Structured ClipSpec output
