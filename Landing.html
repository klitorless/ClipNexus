Why ClipNexus

Built for long-form VOD analysis.

Long streams contain useful moments, but finding and structuring those moments shouldn't require manually searching through hours of footage.

01

Structured Transcripts

Normalize transcript data into a canonical model while preserving timestamps, source information, and uncertainty.

02

Evidence First

Separate what the source actually provides from information derived during processing and analysis.

03

Provider Agnostic

Transcript acquisition is isolated behind provider adapters so the rest of the system does not depend on one service.

Evidence Model

Source data stays separate from derived data.

That separation is foundational to the system. It makes the analysis traceable and helps preserve uncertainty instead of hiding it.

Source Observed

Information directly available from the input or provider.

  • Transcript text
  • Original timestamps
  • Speaker information when available
  • Provider and acquisition provenance
  • Source quality limitations

Derived

Information calculated or interpreted by ClipNexus.

  • Validated transcript structure
  • Evidence relationships
  • Points of Interest
  • Event structures
  • Future ClipSpec definitions
Workflow

A pipeline instead of a pile of features.

Each stage has a defined responsibility and feeds structured data into the next stage.

1

Acquire

Obtain transcript data through supported providers or imported transcript files.

2

Normalize

Convert provider-specific data into the canonical TranscriptDocument model.

3

Validate

Check timestamps, ordering, structure, and source limitations before analysis.

4

Analyze

Extract transcript-supported evidence and relationships without inventing missing information.

5

Structure

Convert evidence into POIs, events, and eventually structured ClipSpec instructions for a future editing system.

AI-assisted. Human-controlled.

ClipNexus is designed to use AI for extracting and describing evidence — not for making the final publishing decision. The system preserves the information needed for a human to review, verify, and choose.
